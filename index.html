Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about testing, fasting, and doorstep phlebotomy.

Do I need a prior appointment for blood tests at the clinic?
No appointment is required for walk-ins at our Tokarkhada center. We are open from 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM (Mon–Sat) and 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM on Sundays. For doorstep home sample collection, we recommend booking the evening before or early morning to secure your preferred morning time slot.
How fast will I receive my diagnostic test reports?
Over 90% of routine tests (CBC, Blood Sugar, Lipid Profile, Liver & Kidney panels, Urine Routine) are reported on the same day. As soon as the consultant pathologist signs off, your official PDF report is automatically sent to your registered WhatsApp and email.
Which localities in Silvassa do you cover for home sample collection?
Our phlebotomy team covers all major residential and industrial areas including Tokarkhada, Samarvarni, Amli, Silvassa City, Masat, Naroli, Piparia, and Dadra. All samples are transported in strict temperature-controlled cold-chain boxes.
Are Avexa's medical reports certified for factory fitness and employment?
Yes. Avexa Healthcare conducts certified pre-employment and statutory annual medical checkups compliant with the Gujarat & Dadra and Nagar Haveli Factory Act. Form 32 and Form 33 health registers are signed by certified certifying surgeons.
