About Avexa Healthcare & Path Labs

Pioneering clinical precision, trusted medical diagnostics, and accessible healthcare solutions in Tokarkhada, Silvassa.

Our Identity & Heritage

A Legacy of Accuracy, Empathy, and Scientific Rigor in Silvassa

Avexa Healthcare & Path Labs was founded to bridge the critical gap between high-end metropolitan diagnostic quality and local community accessibility in Dadra & Nagar Haveli. Situated conveniently on the 3rd Floor, Behind Abuli Hardware, Near ST Bus Depot in Tokarkhada, our laboratory serves hundreds of patients daily alongside major industrial corporations in Silvassa, Naroli, Piparia, and Vapi.

We understand that behind every blood vial, serum sample, and biopsy specimen lies a human being—a worried mother, an expectant parent, an industrial technician, or an elderly grandfather awaiting answers. That is why our team never treats samples as mere numbers; we ensure rigorous dual-check verification on every parameter.
