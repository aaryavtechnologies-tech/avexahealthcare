Patient Advisory

How to Prepare for Your Diagnostic Blood Test

Following simple preparation steps guarantees the highest physiological accuracy for your test results.

01

Fasting Instructions

For Lipid Profile, Fasting Blood Sugar, and Full Body Checkups, fast strictly for 10 to 12 hours overnight. Plain water is permitted and encouraged.

02

Thyroid Medication

Take your thyroid medication only after having your blood drawn in the morning, unless specifically directed otherwise by your endocrinologist.

03

Urine Samples

For Urine Routine or Culture, provide a clean-catch midstream sample using the sterile container provided by Avexa lab or phlebotomist.

04

Post Prandial (PPBS)

The blood sample must be drawn exactly 2 hours after you finish your meal. Note the exact time of your first bite.
